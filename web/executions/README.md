# web/executions

The execution routes: the dispatch, the detail, the steps (the executed-path poll), the resume, the cancel. The badge (the 2s poll fragment) and the badge/row models it renders live in the `badge` subpackage.

## Rules (decisions · pinned values)

- The seven routes are registered in one place (`Register`): `POST /run/{name}` · `GET /status/{id}` · `GET /executions/{id}` · `GET /executions/{id}/steps` · `GET /executions/{id}/steps/{step}` (the expand fragment: the step's stored output/error as a swappable `<pre>`) · `POST /resume/{id}` · `POST /cancel/{id}`. *(evidence: `register.go · Register`)*
- The 2s poll is HTMX-side: the badge fragment and the steps fragment carry `hx-trigger="every 2s"` while the run is live; a terminal badge and a terminal fragment are static — the poll stops. The steps fragment is the v6.3 split (the meta row, the actions, the step log with the child blocks and the dashed tail, and the sticky executed path) — re-rendered every 2s while live (the live elapsed, the tail; `handleExecutionSteps` reads `p.Detail` per poll), and the fragment root is the split itself. The actions live in the fragment, and their ACK still targets `#badge`. *(evidence: `templates/badge.html` · `templates/steps.html` · `badge/badge.go · HandleBadge` · `steps.go · handleExecutionSteps`)*
- The dispatch: form values → `spec.Sanitize` (400 on values that do not sanitize) → `p.Enqueue` → the ACK execrow fragment (the browser prepends it; the badge starts the 2s poll). *(evidence: `dispatch.go · handleRunDispatch` · `formValues`)*
- The meta row: `created` · `elapsed` (live: `ElapsedLabel(now − created)` at render — re-rendered every poll) · `input: name=value` per stored input (the cell is omitted when there is no input) · `retries: attempts − 1` (the count only — no attempts rows; the final attempt is the badge, not a row). *(evidence: `detail.go · newStepsView` · `steps.html`)*
- The cancel ACK (the cancelled badge, terminal) replaces the badge. The resume ACK is the row read back (the same id, enqueued again). *(evidence: `cancel.go · handleCancel` · `resume.go · handleResume`)*
- The child block under the child row: the child's id as a deep link, the child's badge fragment (polling `GET /status/{child-id}` while the child is live), and the child's completed steps (one per-run `Steps` read per child). *(evidence: `steps.html` · `detail.go · newStepsView`)*
